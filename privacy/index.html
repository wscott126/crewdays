Crew Days privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

In short: Crew Days does not collect your log or anything about you. Your log stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics and no ads.

What the app stores

The places and dates you enter, your settings, and any evidence photos or files you add are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives your data and no way of seeing it.

Location

Crew Days never uses your phone’s location. It only knows what you type in yourself.

Backups and exports

If you make a backup file, a PDF report or a spreadsheet, it is created on your phone and you choose where it goes (for example email, Google Drive or iCloud). Once you share it, the service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications (optional): for the reminders and deadline alerts you switch on. They are scheduled on your phone.
  • Camera and photos (optional): only when you add evidence to the evidence folder. Photos are copied into the app on your phone.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional): for the app lock. Your phone checks your fingerprint or face; the app never sees or stores it.

App updates

To get small fixes quickly, the app checks our update service (run by Expo) for a newer version when it opens. This check sends only technical details: the app version, your phone’s operating system, and a random number created for this installation so the service can tell installations apart. That number is not linked to your name, email or phone, and nothing from your log is ever sent. Like any web request, the check comes from your internet connection’s address.

App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store handle downloads, updates and any purchases. They may collect information under their own privacy policies. We receive only the anonymous summary statistics and crash reports they provide to all developers.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

Crew Days is meant for working adults and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
